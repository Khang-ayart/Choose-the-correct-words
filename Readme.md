📚 Choose The Correct Words

Your Personal English Vocabulary Companion

> 🚧 **Alpha: this app is under self-testing.** It is currently being tried out by its developer only. Content, features and the interface may change, break or be removed without notice, and no public release is available yet. More updates will follow.

Choose The Correct Words is a free, offline-first English vocabulary lookup application designed to help users explore words, understand their meanings, and discover the subtle differences between similar expressions.

More than just a dictionary — a tool for understanding how English words are actually used.

✨ Features

- Word Lookup: Search for English words and explore their meanings.
- Pronunciation: View IPA transcriptions and pronunciation information.
- Parts of Speech: Identify nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and other grammatical categories.
- Meaning & Context: Explore different meanings of a word depending on its context.
- Synonyms & Antonyms: Discover related and opposite words.
- Nuance Comparison: Compare similar words and understand differences in meaning, tone, register, and usage.
- Example Sentences: See how words are used in context, accompanied by Vietnamese translations.
- Idioms & Expressions: Explore common idioms and phrases.
- Slang & Abbreviations: Discover informal language, internet expressions, and abbreviations.
- Context-aware Translation: Translations aim to preserve the original meaning, tone, and level of formality instead of automatically making expressions more polite.
- Age-based Categories: Content categories are organized according to the age group selected by the user.

🔒 Privacy & Offline Usage

The application is designed to be lightweight and straightforward.

- No advertisements.
- No mandatory account registration.
- No subscription fees.
- No payment required to access core features.
- No background activity required for ordinary vocabulary lookup.
- Core vocabulary lookup works offline.

Some future features, if introduced, may require an internet connection. Any such changes will be documented.

⚠️ Age Restrictions & Parental Guidance

IMPORTANT: THIS APPLICATION MAY CONTAIN MATURE LANGUAGE, ADULT SLANG, VULGAR EXPRESSIONS, AND OTHER CONTENT INTENDED ONLY FOR ADULT USERS (18+).

The application asks users to select their month and year of birth before accessing its age-based interface.

Certain content categories are restricted to adult users.

Users under 18 must not access adult-only categories.

For children and younger users:

- A parent or legal guardian should review the application before installation.
- Installation and initial setup should be performed or supervised by a responsible adult.
- Parents or guardians should select and supervise the appropriate age category.
- Children should only use content suitable for their age.

The birth-date selection system is a content-access precaution, not a legally certified age-verification system.

Users are responsible for providing accurate age information and complying with the application's age restrictions.

The developer does not encourage minors to access adult-only content.

📖 Content Accuracy Disclaimer

This application is an independent vocabulary-learning project.

Definitions, translations, pronunciations, example sentences, and linguistic explanations are provided for educational and reference purposes.

Language is highly contextual. A word may have different meanings, levels of formality, or implications depending on the situation.

Although efforts are made to provide accurate information, errors, incomplete definitions, or translation inconsistencies may occur.

The developer does not guarantee that every entry is completely accurate or suitable for every context.

Users should use their own judgment when applying vocabulary and expressions in real-world communication.
